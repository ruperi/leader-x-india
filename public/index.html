THE METHOD

Zoom out. See the signal.

A rules-based research screen for completed monthly candles. Not a buy or sell recommendation.

What do these signals mean?

RSI 14 uses Wilder smoothing on completed monthly closes. Fresh Signals are symbols with previous RSI ≤ 70 and current RSI > 70. Strong Momentum is current RSI > 70 excluding fresh signals. Neutral is RSI 69.3–70 inclusive. Exit Watch is RSI < 69.3: an alert for review, not advice. Read signal dates alongside the latest completed month.

How are breakout and stop references used?

A breakout is based on a later monthly high exceeding the signal candle's high; it does not mean today's price is above it. The previous month's low is a stop reference, not a guaranteed execution price or loss limit. Gaps and slippage can occur.

What should I know about the data?

Demo rows use fictional symbols and synthetic illustrations, not actual stock history or performance. Live data comes from Upstox. Corporate-action adjustment is unverified: splits and dividends can affect the series and RSI. No future returns are promised.
